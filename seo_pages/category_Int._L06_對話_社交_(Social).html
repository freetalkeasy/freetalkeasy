很榮幸認識你。It's an honor to meet you.

我來自台灣。I come from Taiwan.
你住在哪裡？Where do you live?
我住在台北。I live in Taipei.
你做什麼工作？What do you do for a living?
我是一名工程師。I am an engineer.
我還是個學生。I am still a student.

我是獨生子。I am an only child.

我還單身。I am still single.

我有兩個孩子。I have two children.

我喜歡旅行和看書。I like traveling and reading.

我喜歡打籃球。I like playing basketball.

你的母語是什麼？What is your native language?
我正在學習日文。I am learning Japanese.

我喜歡聽音樂。I like listening to music.
