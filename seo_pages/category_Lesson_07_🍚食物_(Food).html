我們需要一點時間看菜單。We need some time to look at the menu.
請問有什麼推薦的招牌菜嗎？Are there any recommended signature dishes?
我想要點和這個一樣的。I would like to order the same as this.
這道菜會很辣嗎？Is this dish very spicy?
請幫我把醬汁分開放。Please put the sauce on the side.
我對花生嚴重過敏。I have a severe allergy to peanuts.
這樣就夠了，先點這些。That is enough for now, I will order these first.
請問我們的餐點還要等多久？How much longer do we have to wait for our food?
這道菜我們沒有點。We did not order this dish.
請再給我們一些餐巾紙。Please give us some more napkins.
可以幫我加滿水嗎？Could you refill my water, please?
我想要加點一份甜點。I would like to order an extra dessert.
麻煩幫我把剩下的打包。Please pack the leftovers for me.
請問可以在桌邊結帳嗎？Can we pay at the table?
請分開結帳，謝謝。Please split the bill, thank you.
這個帳單好像有點錯誤。There seems to be a mistake on this bill.
今天的餐點非常美味。The meal today was very delicious.
請問有靠窗的座位嗎？Are there any window seats available?
我們沒有先訂位。We do not have a reservation.
我們準備好要點餐了。We are ready to order.
請問這個有素食的版本嗎？Is there a vegetarian version of this?
請幫我的飲料去冰。Please make my drink without ice.
這份沙拉不需要加沙拉醬。No dressing is needed for this salad.
可以幫我催一下菜嗎？Could you check on our order, please?
我不小心把叉子弄掉了。I accidentally dropped my fork.
